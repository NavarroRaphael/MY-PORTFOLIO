Explanation of each tag

Bold Text: Makes text bold.

Important Text: Indicates important text.

Italic Text: Makes text italic.

Emphasized Text: Emphasizes text.

Underlined Text: Underlines text.

Highlighted Text: Highlights text.

Smaller Text: Makes text smaller.

Deleted Text: Shows deleted text.

Inserted Text: Shows inserted text.

Strikethrough Text: Strikes through text.

Subscript Text: Makes text subscript.

Superscript Text: Makes text superscript.
